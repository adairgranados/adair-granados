# Week 08: DataViz II: Storytelling, decluttering, and accessibility
<!-- /gen -->

*Remember: Your [Associate](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-associate) can draft a chart quickly. This week you learn to edit that draft into a figure that gives your client something to act on.*


## Overview

In **[Quest 08](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-quest): The Boardroom Story**, you turn four exploratory charts into one slide for a CFO.


## This week's learning objectives

By the end of Week 8 you can:

1. Rewrite a descriptive chart title as a takeaway title that states the finding
2. Apply the declutter sequence to an AI-drafted chart by directing your Associate one step at a time. Check that nothing you delete carries information
3. Annotate a figure: Mark the events, name the differences, and put the "so what" on the chart
4. Choose colour by meaning (sequential, diverging, or qualitative). Default to the Okabe-Ito colour-blind-safe palette, and do not rely on colour alone
5. Choose Truth! axes: Zero baselines for bars, the <!-- plain-language: everyday intent --> intent test for line charts, and alternatives to dual y-axes
6. Write one-sentence alt text (chart type, variables, and takeaway) for any figure you ship
7. Decide when an interactive (hvPlot) chart serves the reader better than a static figure
