# Week 07: Independent practice week
<!-- /gen -->

*There are no classes this week. Your team holds its group project [Sprint Zero](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-sprint-zero) on its own.*


## Overview

In one session, your team audits the fire-brigade tables, logs its first cleaning <!-- plain-language: everyday decision -->decisions, and gives each task an owner (the person responsible) and a delivery date. You do not submit anything this week.


## This week's learning objectives

By the end of Week 7 you can:

1. Profile an assigned slice of the London Fire Brigade incident and mobilisation tables: Shape, types, missing values, duplicates, category consistency, and whether the tables join. Report the findings to your team
2. Turn audit findings into logged [`DECISION:` entries](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-decision-entry) that state what was decided, why, and who owns the action
3. Run the audit → <!-- plain-language: everyday decision -->decision → clean pipeline from the [Demonstration](./Demonstration.ipynb). Adapt each step to the fire-brigade tables, including the join on `IncidentNumber` and the challenges in the [data note](../../Assessment/lfb-data.md)
4. Allocate project tasks with one named owner and one date each, and schedule the weekly [stand-up](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-stand-up) your team keeps until submission

If your team has time after the sprint:

5. Show a first draft of at least one figure the group report will need, with the question it answers written above it

- [ ] Read the [Introduction](./Introduction.ipynb), and work through its section "Before you arrive" (about 1 hour)

## At home (about 2.5 hours, before Week 8)

- [ ] Read the [Week 6 Debrief](../06/Debrief.ipynb) first, including the [AI failure gallery](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-ai-failure-gallery) ... and a little extra ...

- [ ] **Complete your allocated tasks** by the dates your team agreed. Add your individual coding sessions to your [Prompt Diary](../../Guides/glossary.md#term-prompt-diary)
- [ ] **Hold your first post-sprint stand-up** at the time you agreed on, with the [weekly agenda](../../Guides/stoa-workflow.md#3-the-weekly-stand-up)
- [ ] Reflect on your prompts: Open your Prompt Diary and run the [REFLECT prompt](../../Guides/prompts.md#reflect-on-your-prompts) (about 10 min)
